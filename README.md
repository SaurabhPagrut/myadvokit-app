# MyAdvoKit: free legal tools for lawyers and advocates in India

**[Open MyAdvoKit →](https://myadvokit.saurabhpagrut.in/)**

[![MyAdvoKit: free law tools for advocates in India](https://myadvokit.saurabhpagrut.in/og.png)](https://myadvokit.saurabhpagrut.in/)

MyAdvoKit is a free web app for litigation advocates, lawyers, their clerks and juniors, and law students in India. It can:

- keep your matters and hearing dates, with reminders;
- help prepare a case with limitation, interest and court fee calculators, a list of dates and events, and IPC to BNS lookup;
- make court-ready **e-Filing PDF bundles** with a bilingual index, running page numbers and bookmarks.

It runs in your browser and works offline. **Your case files are never uploaded.** It's free, needs no account, and works in English, मराठी and हिन्दी.

> MyAdvoKit (myadvokit.saurabhpagrut.in, formerly AbogadaFlow) is an independent Indian legal-tech web app built by [Saurabh Pagrut](https://saurabhpagrut.in/). It is **not** affiliated with Advokit (myadvokit.ai), AdvoKIT or Advokite.

This repository is the project's public home page. The app's source code is private.

## Why advocates use it

- **e-Filing without scrutiny objections.** It counts the pages, types the index, stamps continuous page numbers and adds a bookmark for every section, so the registry doesn't send the bundle back.
- **Private by design.** Files are read and changed inside your browser, on your own device. The site's security policy only lets the page talk to its own address, so the browser itself blocks files from being sent anywhere else.
- **Built for Maharashtra and Indian courts.** Presets for the Bombay High Court, Maharashtra district courts and tribunals. Indexes in English, Marathi, Hindi or a mix. Marathi OCR for FIRs, panchnamas and 7/12 extracts. Court fees under the Maharashtra and Karnataka Acts.
- **Works in court with no signal.** Install it on your phone or laptop and save every tool for offline use.

## Install it

- **Android:** open [the site](https://myadvokit.saurabhpagrut.in/) in Chrome and choose **Install app**.
- **iPhone:** open it in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Windows or Mac:** use the install icon in the Chrome or Edge address bar.

Then open **Settings → Data** and press **Save every tool for offline use**.

## Questions

**Is it free?** Yes. Every tool is free, with no account or sign-up.

**How do I make an e-Filing bundle with an index and page numbers?** Open the [bundle tool](https://myadvokit.saurabhpagrut.in/bundle) and pick a court preset. Drop in each section as its own PDF, check the index titles and press **Compile bundle**. You get one PDF with a bilingual index, running page numbers and a bookmark for every section.

**Which courts does it support?** It comes with presets for the Bombay High Court, Maharashtra district courts and tribunals. The presets are starting points: you can change the page number position, index numbering and headings to match your court.

**How do I keep my cases if I change phones?** Open Settings → Data and save a full backup, locked with your password. Then restore it on the new device.

**Does it give legal advice?** No. It helps with the paperwork. Check every section, limitation period and fee against the official text before you rely on it.
